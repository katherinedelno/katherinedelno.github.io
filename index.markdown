    <h2>How I work</h2>
    <p>Most recurring errors have a cause a step or two earlier than where they show up, and finding that cause is most of my job. So we solve problems together, where I can see the setup, the notation, and the hesitations, and then I step back and let the student work alone, because that is the only honest test of what has stuck. I keep the roster small on purpose: the instruction gets more precise the longer I know a student's work.</p>
