{%- comment -%} Email signup. Three modes, checked in order: 1. site.newsletter_action — a provider endpoint (Buttondown, Kit, Formspree). 2. site.google_form_id — posts to your own Google Form, invisibly. The response lands in a hidden iframe so the page never navigates away, and the visitor sees our own confirmation rather than Google's. 3. neither set — renders an RSS link and a mailto, so no address is ever collected into nowhere.
