keyboard: tab to the scatterplot · comma and period choose a point · arrow keys move it · shift-arrows move faster · a adds a point · delete removes it

Drag any point and watch the line, the residual plot, and the statistics respond together. Click empty space to add a point. When a point is selected, the dashed line shows the fit computed without it; the gap between the two lines is that point's influence. The line is drawn gray beyond the range of the data, where it makes no supported prediction.
